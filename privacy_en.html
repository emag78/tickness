1. Local data and installation registration

Tasks, categories and preferences are normally stored on your device. Unshared tasks are not automatically sent to Sync, but may be transferred if you choose a backup, a link or an export.

From version 2.13.2+100, your device is registered on AWS only when needed for Sync, including a pending request to join a channel, or when you consent to optional statistics. Using local features alone does not require a remote profile.

The profile includes a persistent random ID, which is pseudonymous, not anonymous; a technical or chosen device name; platform, app version and build; registration, update, activity and expiry dates; statistics preference and an indication of previous channel creation. A chosen name may contain personal data. The recorded version is the last one reported, not necessarily the current version.

These data identify the device, support Sync, compatibility and assistance or, with consent, statistics. To protect access to online features, Tickness uses securely stored technical credentials. Registration does not ask for your legal name or email. The ID is not an advertising identifier and is not used for advertising, selling data or commercial profiling.

When neither Sync nor statistics needs the profile, the app requests its deletion. If Sync is still needed, withdrawing consent stops statistics but retains the profile. The local ID and credential remain on the device. See section 10 for timing and exceptions.

2. Microphone, dictation and contacts

If you dictate a note, speech recognition may send audio to its service provider, depending on your device and settings. Tickness requests the necessary permissions and does not send audio to its own backend. Transcribed text is treated like other notes.

You can insert selected contact details, such as a name, phone number or email, into notes. Your address book is not automatically uploaded to Tickness; inserted details may be included in sharing and backups you choose.

3. Calendar, maps and other services

Exporting a task to a calendar passes its included title, dates, notes and place to your chosen service, which may synchronise them with your account. Maps, links and sharing tools receive selected information under their own privacy rules. Tickness does not automatically collect GPS location: places come from entered or imported data.

On iOS and Android, update checks send Apple or Google services an app-related request and network data, such as your IP address, without tasks or the Installation ID.

4. Google Drive and iCloud backups

Google Drive and iCloud backups are optional and separate from Sync. They are stored in your personal account, not a central Tickness archive. Google Drive gives the app access only to files created by or authorised for it, not your entire Drive. To restore a backup, the app retrieves the copy from your chosen account.

Backups may include tasks, categories and, for shared channels, member information and decryption keys. Tickness does not add application-level encryption to the file before upload. Copies go to your chosen personal service, not the AWS backend. The app locally stores account and latest-backup references, such as email, identifiers and date.

You can manage and delete copies through Google Drive or iCloud, subject to their rules on versions and trash. Changing or disconnecting accounts, or uninstalling Tickness, does not automatically delete existing backups.

5. Link sharing

Generating a link, even just to copy it, sends selected tasks to AWS: titles, notes, checklists, categories, dates, places, recurrence and other included data. Transfers are protected, but contents do not use Sync’s application-level encryption and are readable by the service.

Anyone with the link can access and import its contents while available. If you use WhatsApp, the prepared message and selected titles are also sent to that service.

The scheduled expiry is 30 days from creation. Deletion is asynchronous: a link may remain usable until actual removal.

6. Synchronisation and shared channels

Sync is optional. It keeps shared tasks and categories aligned between authorised devices, storing encrypted contents and necessary metadata on AWS. Changes propagate within channels: Sync is not a separate personal backup. Authorised participants can read and retain copies of shared data.

Main task contents, such as titles, notes, checklists, scheduling and places, are encrypted on the device before transmission. The service can still read identifiers, statuses, dates, versions and authors of changes, category associations and settings, and category, channel and device names. Memberships, invitations and join requests are also processed.

Connections are protected. Task encryption does not cover all metadata, links or personal backups.

7. Optional statistics and diagnostics

Statistics are OFF by default and require explicit consent. They are independent of Sync: declining them does not limit Sync or local features. You can change your choice in Settings at any time.

With consent, we collect daily and overall counts covering device registration and management, channel and invitation use, join requests, service limits, and changes or removal of shared tasks and categories. We also count active devices and channels. Task text is not used for these statistics; they are not a complete record of app actions or launches.

Daily markers include installation or channel IDs, event types, counts and dates. Aggregate totals are separate and do not list individual IDs, but are not necessarily anonymous in every context. In shared channels, a count may result from a consenting participant’s action without indicating every member’s consent.

Activation waits for service confirmation. Withdrawal is saved immediately and stops optional participation on the device, even offline. If the service cannot be reached, the app retains your choice and retries the update; some errors need support. The remote service may temporarily retain the previous choice and finish processing already started. Withdrawal does not automatically delete historical counts.

Operational and security logs, and metrics on requests, errors, performance and costs, remain separate, including with statistics OFF. Providers may process IP addresses and network data; IP addresses are not part of the device profile. No advertising SDKs or external analytics/crash-reporting services are active in the paths reviewed. App notifications are local.

9. Recipients, providers and international transfers

AWS hosts Sync, device profiles, link contents, consented statistics and Tickness’s technical services. The application region is Frankfurt, Germany (EU). AWS is neither your main personal storage nor your backup service. Link recipients and authorised channel participants receive shared data.

For data processed on a customer’s behalf, AWS acts as a processor under its Data Processing Addendum, incorporated into its Service Terms where provided. For its own account and service data, it may act as an independent controller under the AWS Privacy Notice. The European region does not exclude transfers outside the EEA: the public terms provide standard contractual clauses where applicable, unless another recognised mechanism applies. You can ask the controller for information and copies of relevant safeguards.

Gmail is used to handle support and privacy emails, including addresses, messages, attachments and communication data. Google processes service data under its Privacy Policy, acting as a controller for its own purposes. Its infrastructure is global; international transfer safeguards include adequacy decisions, the Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses, depending on the transfer.

Other services you choose: Google Drive, iCloud, calendars, speech recognition, maps and sharing follow their providers’ terms; see also Apple’s Privacy Policy. Personal backups are separate from the controller’s Gmail mailbox. Data may be disclosed to authorities where required by law.

10. Retention and deletion

Automatic expiry determines when data become eligible for removal. Deletion is asynchronous and may occur later.

  • Local data: until removed through the app or system. A local reset does not erase cloud data or other people’s copies.
  • Device profile: 365 days from the last technical update; updates may renew this while Sync or statistics still needs the profile. When no longer needed, the app requests deletion without waiting for expiry.
  • Statistics: daily markers for 7 days. Aggregates and summaries have no general automatic expiry: retention follows the need to analyse service trends and reliability and handle data requests; removal requires assessment and manual action.
  • Links: 30 days from creation.
  • Sync: active channels, memberships and current contents have no general expiry while needed for shared features. Completed or deleted tasks and deleted or unshared categories normally receive 30 days; tasks removed from sharing in a batch receive 90 days. Task histories normally receive 90 days, or 180 for batch removal from sharing. Some category histories follow the final record’s 30 days; others have no general expiry.
  • Permanent channel closure: operational data, including memberships and histories, receive an expiry of 7 days from closure, preserving earlier deadlines. One participant leaving does not close the channel.
  • Other technical data: invitations and join requests follow their own validity periods; technical expiry may differ from how long they remain retrievable. Temporary connections: 2 days. Reviewed Tickness application logs: 7 days, without extending this period to every infrastructure log. Administrative audits: 90 days from creation, without renewal, for channel and task references, technical hashes, counts and dates.
  • Personal backups: under Google Drive or iCloud settings and rules, including versions and trash; the app imposes no expiry.
  • Emails: to handle requests and follow-up; afterwards, only as needed for applicable obligations or legal claims. Removal is manual under Gmail’s rules.

Offline operation, authentication errors or cloud memberships still marked active may delay profile deletion; contact the controller if the problem persists. Profile deletion does not automatically erase Sync contents, participants’ copies or other references to the ID. Data without automatic expiry are retained for as long as needed to operate the service and can be removed manually, while respecting users’ rights.

No additional backups are configured within the AWS features examined. Any provider infrastructure copies are separate from personal backups: the periods above do not ensure their simultaneous removal. Erasure or restriction requests must also be considered after a restoration.

11. Purposes and legal bases

  • Sync, links and other requested features: performance of the service, GDPR Article 6(1)(b), for necessary data, including registration needed for Sync.
  • Optional statistics and associated registration: consent, Article 6(1)(a). Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.
  • Security, logs and reliability: legitimate interests in protecting the service, preventing abuse and handling errors, Article 6(1)(f), subject to necessity and proportionality.
  • Rights requests and legal obligations: Article 6(1)(c), for obligations that apply.

Statistics consent is separate from device permissions and Sync. Legitimate interests do not replace any consent required to access information on your device. We do not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

12. Your rights and erasure requests

Email enrico.magrella@gmail.com to exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection rights where provided by the GDPR. You can withdraw statistics consent in Settings and complain to the Italian data protection authority or the competent supervisory authority.

We normally respond within one month and explain any justified extension. You can provide the Installation ID from Settings to help locate data; do not send credentials, Sync keys or complete backups. Identity checks are proportionate.

Withdrawing statistics consent does not erase all data: specify your request if you seek broader erasure. We also consider applicable obligations and other participants’ rights; the ID alone cannot locate every detail in encrypted contents. Objections to legitimate-interest processing are assessed in light of your situation and GDPR Article 21.

13. Choices, earlier versions and policy updates

Sync, statistics, backups, links and external services are separate choices. Declining a permission may prevent the related feature, but local features work without Sync or statistics.

Versions before 2.13.2+100 may register devices automatically. The updated app applies the new conditions when it can update the service; older clients may still send requests. Profiles of devices no longer used may remain until expiry or a specific request. Uninstalling or not reopening the app does not itself request remote deletion.

This policy is available on tickness.com and in the app. Material changes will be communicated appropriately.
