Tickness is more than a simple calendar. It is a space designed to clear your mind and bring clarity to your daily organization.

Here’s how it helps:

  • 📌 Collect everything you need to remember in one place.
  • 🗓️ See upcoming commitments at a glance (within 14 days).
  • 🧭 Keep track of “to be scheduled” tasks without losing them.
  • 🌱 Plan future activities with calm and without mental overload.
  • ✔️ Review what you’ve completed to measure your actual progress.
  • 🔗 Link each task to its resources: documents, videos, audio, or external links, always accessible directly from the task.
  • 🤖 Organize with the Tickness Assistant, a GPT tool that helps you clarify tasks, deadlines and priorities.

You can try the Tickness Assistant directly in your browser:
chatgpt.com/tickness-assistant

Tickness combines the precision of a tick with the mindful clarity of presence: less noise, more mental space.
